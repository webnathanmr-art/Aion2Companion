※ Efficiency = effective damage gained per 1%p ÷ the multiplier already stacked on that stat. The more a stat is already stacked, the less the same 1%p is felt.
※ Flat attack power gets different multipliers depending on which "pocket" it lands in — Manastones/Enchants (Gear Attack: Weapon Dmg Amp × Increase Rate), Pet Bonus Attack (Base Attack: Increase Rate only), Pet Max Attack (half the value counts as Gear Attack), PVE Attack (no multiplier at all).
※ Smite % is the stat-window value; after adding the party buff and subtracting boss resistance, the result is the actual proc rate applied.
※ Attack Increase Rate is auto-calculated from Destruction (0.2%/pt) · Power (0.1%/pt) · Gear Breakthrough (+5%/slot); the breakthrough portion is bound to the gear itself, so it's excluded from the rate when gear is removed.

Your stat-window Combat Power (전투력) is computed server-side by NCsoft, mostly from gear item level/enhancement — and NCsoft has openly re-weighted it more than once (a 2026 patch raised the CP weight of Smite/Accuracy/Critical and lowered Penetration/Spirit's weight, and players reported their CP number dropping overnight as a result). Separately, the Korean community runs 아툴 (Atool, aion2tool.com) — a from-scratch DPS-estimate score built from real skill-damage-share data, which parties actually use to judge a player's contribution, because two characters at the same Combat Power can put out very different real damage depending on their Soul Engraving rolls (random substats every piece of gear carries). Independent Proc Stats — Smite, Perfect, Multi-Hit

2×
Smite (강타 / 强击)
Roughly doubles the hit's damage when it procs — the single biggest per-proc multiplier in the game, bigger than a Critical (~1.5×). Wisdom [Lumiel] pantheon points convert directly to Smite chance at +0.2%/point.
~3%
Perfect (완벽 / 完美)
Locks the hit's damage to your weapon's maximum attack roll instead of a random point in its range. Measured average DPS gain is small (~3%) since it only matters for the width of your weapon's attack range.
94%
Multi-Hit (다단히트 / 多段打击)
Base auto-attacks already proc a multi-hit roll ~94% of the time, cascading up to 4 extra hits (reported distribution ≈ 50% for 0 extra hits, 25% for 1, 12.5% for 2, and a combined ~12.5% for 3–4). Each extra hit that lands adds roughly +20% final damage — and unlike Power/Precision, this doesn't run into an overflow cap.
※ These three roll independently per hit — they don't replace each other, they stack as separate chances on top of your base Critical roll.

Itemization Pipeline — 5 systems that build your gear

1
Base Enhancement (기본강화)
Standard gear upgrade currency/materials — the floor everyone does first.
2
Breakthrough (돌파강화)
Each breakthrough on a piece is worth +5% to your Attack Increase Rate (see Damage Efficiency tab) — but it's bound to the gear, so it's lost if that piece is removed/replaced.
3
Manastone / Spiritstone Engraving (마석·영석 각인)
Socketed stat gems — flat Attack Power, Damage Amp %, etc. Casual-friendly baseline: push Weapon/Sub-weapon to +15 breakthrough / 2-star first.
4
Godstone Engraving (신석각인)
On-hit proc effects (stun, seal/silence-lock, paralyze, silence) at varying proc rate/duration by grade — crowd-control utility, not a raw damage stat, and doesn't stack with itself.
5
Soul Engraving (영혼각인)
A random bonus substat every piece of gear carries regardless of its main stats — see the dedicated card above. The single biggest swing factor in real performance past ~2,500 CP.

Transcendence (초월) Dungeons — 10-stage endgame content, difficulty scales per stage

01
Shattered Arcanis — Boss: Talisra
Wipe-mechanic phases trigger at ~60% and ~30% HP. Blue and purple orbs spawn at the map edges and drift toward the boss; assign 2 players to each color before the pull. Collecting an orb grants a shield of that color — if everyone chases one color, the opposite shield never forms and the raid wipes.
02
Deus Research Base — Boss: Artiel
Fast-paced boss that mixes patterns quickly. At certain HP thresholds it destroys the map's outer edge, shrinking the arena and increasing hazard density; its knockback attacks can then knock you off the map. From stage 4 onward it alternates two contradictory chain mechanics — "You can't escape me" (move away from the boss) and "Bound in chains" (move toward/stick to the boss) — reading the callout correctly matters more than reflexes here.
Shattered Arcanis: assign 2 players to blue and 2 to purple before the pull. Orbs drift inward from the map edge; each one collected raises a shield of that colour.
Deus Research Base: two chain callouts that demand opposite reactions. The arena also shrinks at HP thresholds, so knockbacks near the edge become lethal.
※ Higher Transcendence stages (1–10) scale monster stats, add dungeon-wide weather effects, and give named bosses additional/stronger patterns on top of the above.

GS
Gear Score / Item Level
The gatekeeper. Content checks this to let you in — item level ~1,000 is the threshold for finishing the story and entering the first endgame dungeons. You can be gear-score-eligible and still be too weak.
CP
Combat Power
Your actual power, and what other players judge you by. See the Combat Power Breakdown tab for what actually raises it — and why it can disagree with your real damage.

PvP Systems

1
The Abyss — open-world faction warfare
Aion's signature PvP space: a three-dimensional zone where Elyos and Asmodians fight over territory, contested world bosses and siege-style objectives on repeatable reward loops. Note the structural change from the original Aion — factions meet in designated areas/modes rather than through the old massive fortress sieges.
2
Rifting — invading enemy territory
Rift events temporarily open passages into normally-safe enemy zones. Aion 2 adds a War Mode toggle: entering a rift defaults to PvE safety, and you manually flag War Mode to become attackable (and able to attack).
3
Arenas — instanced, rated
Structured formats with queuing, matchmaking and rating. Arena of Solitude is the 1v1 format. Gear is not equalized — progression and optimization carry directly into arena results.
4
Abyss Points (AP) — the PvP currency
The main PvP currency, and the only way to buy PvP gear. Weekly AP earning is capped (PvE hunting and PvP share the same allowance).
※ Ranking tracks three separate things: individual Abyss rank (total AP accumulated), per-format Arena rating, and seasonal rankings across all formats.
※ For PvP stat priority specifically, note from the Combat Power tab that Critical Damage is far weaker in PvP (1.25× base multiplier instead of PvE's 1.5×, and the Critical Damage stat is only ~15% as effective), and that the Indomitability Arcana set is the PvP-defensive pick.
